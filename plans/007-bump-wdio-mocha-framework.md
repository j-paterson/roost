## Why this matters

`npm audit` reports **3 high-severity advisories** (minimatch ReDoS,
serialize-javascript, js-yaml prototype pollution). All three come in
transitively through **one stale dev dependency**: `@wdio/mocha-framework` is
pinned at `^7.7.3` while the entire rest of the WebdriverIO stack is `^9.27.0`.
The v7 framework bundles `mocha@8.1.3`, which pulls the vulnerable
`minimatch@<3.1.4` and friends. The exposure is dev/CI-only (the test runner,
not the shipped plugin), but it nags every `npm install` and blocks a clean
audit. Bumping `@wdio/mocha-framework` to `^9` aligns it with the stack and
replaces the vulnerable transitive tree.
